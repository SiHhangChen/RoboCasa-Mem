# Third-party dependencies and assets

MobileMemBench code follows the repository's MIT license. RoboCasa and
robosuite are separate dependencies; their MIT notices and third-party terms
remain applicable. MuJoCo is separately licensed under Apache 2.0.

Kitchen, object, mesh, and texture assets are obtained through RoboCasa's
asset downloader and the existing separately supplied archive inventoried in
`configs/benchmark/simulator_assets.json`. The benchmark MIT license does not grant additional rights
to third-party assets. No simulator asset bundle or pretrained checkpoint is
included in this candidate. Consult the pinned simulator repositories and
asset source notices before redistributing assets.

The candidate includes the file inventory and validation evidence, not the
archive itself. The inventory's hashes identify content and do not grant
redistribution rights. Acquisition and restoration are described in `docs/ASSETS.md`.
